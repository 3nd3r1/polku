# Equal Care Across Finland: AstraZeneca

**Track:** Healthcare · **Link:** https://hackjunction.app/participate/45/challenges/344

## Brief

Build an **AI-powered system that supports equal and effective cancer care across Finland**, making sure time to diagnosis is optimal and treatment starts within target timeframes wherever patients live.

Your solution should:
1. **Identify the gap:** differences in access or outcomes.
2. **Locate it in the pathway:** detection → referral (primary → specialist) → diagnosis → treatment start.
3. **Recommend what to do** (**the most important part**): concrete, evidence-based proposals implementable nationally or per wellbeing county.

**Format is open:** an AI analyst that explains indicator trends and proposes actions, a monitoring dashboard with recommendations, an early-warning system for delays, or an intervention impact simulation.

## Minimum requirements

- Reliable evidence: public data and/or mentor insights, **with sources for every claim**
- Cover at least one indicator tied to **Goal 3 of the national cancer strategy**
- Actionable recommendations, not just analysis
- **Working demo.** Slides alone are not enough.

**Known bottlenecks:** late detection, the primary-to-specialist referral, diagnosis-to-treatment time. Finnish cancer data is limited, and working around that gap is part of the challenge. A non-technical decision-maker should get it **in under 2 minutes**. Possible follow-up project with a Finnish wellbeing services county.

## Judging

| Criterion | Weight |
|---|---|
| Actionability (concrete enough for a health centre or county to implement) | 30 % |
| Insight (real gap, located in the pathway) | 25 % |
| Data quality & transparency | 20 % |
| Execution (works live) | 15 % |
| Clarity (decision-maker gets it in 2 min) | 10 % |
