# What the national cancer strategy says

Source: the AstraZeneca resource pack: national cancer strategy 2026-2035, implementation roadmap 2026-2031, data link list (URLs in `reference/challenge-astrazeneca.md`). Page numbers refer to the PDFs.

## Goal 3: "Varmistetaan yhdenvertainen ja vaikuttava syövänhoito"

The brief requires at least one Goal 3 indicator. Headline indicators (strategy p.5):

- **Patients get treatment within target times** ("Potilaat pääsevät hoitoon tavoiteaikojen puitteissa"), monitored under THL guidance Ohjaus 21/2016 *Hoitoonpääsyn seuranta syövän hoidossa*. No numeric target in days. **This is Polku's indicator.**
- 5-year survival of poor-prognosis cancers reaches the Nordic level: **lung +14 pp**, pancreas +6 pp, liver +14 pp vs Nordcan 2023 (Nordic level = mean of Sweden and Norway).
- Cancer mortality down 10 % (now 13 300 deaths a year).
- Regional and socioeconomic differences in incidence, treatment and mortality are visible and used to steer services.

Sub-goals: 3.1 national care guidelines and **care pathways** · 3.2 national cancer quality registry · 3.3 rehabilitation, psychosocial, palliative · 3.4 functional capacity of older patients (G8) · 3.5 staff and **digital tools**.

## Roadmap measures Polku implements

| Measure | What it says | Timeline |
|---|---|---|
| **3.1.2** (roadmap p.56-57) | Cancer-specific national pathway descriptions, modelled on Denmark's Kræftpakkeforløb, Sweden's SVF and Norway's Pakkeforløp. Steps from suspicion to diagnostics, treatment start and follow-up, with **target access times**, quality indicators and coding rules. | 2026-2028: pathways for 5 cancers, access times defined |
| **2.5.2** (roadmap p.52-53) | **Standardised referral criteria and symptom-based models**: when criteria are met, patients with possible cancer symptoms go fast to diagnostics. Direct test routes and fast-track access. | 2026-2028: the 5 most common cancers, pilots in counties |
| **3.5.3** (roadmap p.73-74) | **Digital care pathways**, remote monitoring, **symptom assessment**, self-care apps, AI for early detection. Budget **1.14 M€**. | 2026-2028: survey, national spec, limited pilots |

Also: 70 % of cancers diagnosed at stage I-II by 2035 (Goal 2 indicator, same measure 2.5.2). The strategy calls regional comparison of access times "sporadic and coarse" (p.35).

**Why it matters:** the Nordic pathways are effectively a published ontology: entry criteria, ordered steps, a maximum time per step. Finland has committed to building its own and hasn't yet.
