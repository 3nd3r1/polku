# Cancer challenge: what the strategy docs say

Source: AstraZeneca resource pack (national cancer strategy 2026-2035, implementation roadmap 2026-2031, data link list). Extracted Fri 13.11 19:50. Page numbers refer to the PDFs.

## Goal 3 (the one the brief requires): "Varmistetaan yhdenvertainen ja vaikuttava syövänhoito"

Headline indicators (strategy p.5):
- **Patients get treatment within target times** ("Potilaat pääsevät hoitoon tavoiteaikojen puitteissa"), per THL guidance Ohjaus 21/2016 *Hoitoonpääsyn seuranta syövän hoidossa*
- 5-year survival of poor-prognosis cancers reaches the Nordic level: **lung +14 pp, pancreas +6 pp, liver +14 pp** vs Nordcan 2023 (Nordic level = mean of Sweden and Norway)
- Cancer mortality down 10 % (now 13 300 deaths a year)
- Cross-cutting equality: regional and socioeconomic differences in incidence, treatment and mortality are visible and used to steer services

Sub-goals: 3.1 national care guidelines and **care pathways** drive equal, delay-free care · 3.2 national cancer quality registry · 3.3 rehab, psychosocial, palliative · 3.4 functional capacity data for older patients (G8 screen) · 3.5 staff and **digital tools**

## The measures that match your idea

| Measure | What it says | Timeline |
|---|---|---|
| **3.1.2** (roadmap p.56-57) | Cancer-specific national care pathway descriptions, modelled on **Denmark Kræftpakkeforløb, Sweden SVF, Norway Pakkeforløp**. Steps from suspicion to diagnostics, treatment start and follow-up, with **target access times**, quality indicators and coding rules. | 2026-2028: pathways for 5 cancers, cancer-specific access times defined |
| **2.5.2** (roadmap p.52-53) | **Standardised referral criteria and symptom-based models**: when criteria are met, patients with possible cancer symptoms go fast to diagnostics. Direct test routes and fast-track access. | 2026-2028: in the 5 most common cancers, pilots in counties |
| **3.5.3** (roadmap p.73-74) | **Digital care pathways**, remote monitoring, **symptom assessment**, self-care apps, AI for early detection. Patient safety, usability, accessibility. Budget **1.14 M€**. | 2026-2028: survey, national spec, limited pilots |
| 2.5.1 | More GP capacity, named-GP model (omalääkäri) | |

Also: 70 % of cancers diagnosed at stage I-II by 2035 (Goal 2 indicator, the same measure 2.5.2).

**Why this matters:** Nordic standardised pathways are effectively a published ontology: entry criteria (alarm symptoms → "well-founded suspicion"), ordered steps, and a max lead time per step. Finland has committed to building its own but **hasn't yet**. A prototype of a Finnish pathway ontology with a patient front door and a clock on every step is right on the roadmap.

## Public data sources (from the link list)

- Syöpärekisteri: statistics app, screening stats (incidence, survival, stage per region)
- THL Sotkanet indicator bank (regional indicators)
- Statistics Finland: causes of death (StatFin)
- Kela open data (special reimbursement rights)
- NORDCAN 2.0 (Nordic comparison, the survival gap)
- Findata, FinRegistry, biobanks: need permits, **not usable this weekend**
- The Swedish SVF, Danish and Norwegian pathway definitions are public. They're good sources for criteria and lead times (to verify).

## Gaps to ask AstraZeneca about

1. Are there **public per-county access-time numbers** (referral → diagnosis → treatment), or only national? The strategy says regional comparison is "sporadic and coarse" (p.35).
2. Which of the 5 first pathways do they expect (lung? colorectal? breast? prostate?), and do any Finnish counties already run fast-track pathways?
3. Would a patient-facing symptom front door count as "actionable at county level" if it ships with a county dashboard and an implementation plan?
